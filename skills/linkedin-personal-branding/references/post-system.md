# 60-Day Post System

## Goal

Ship 8 genuinely useful artifacts and make the market watch you do it. The
60 days are a campaign with one through-line — the value ladder toward the
offer — not 60 separate posts. See `references/artifact-system.md` for the
artifact rules and the post quality bar; this file covers sequencing.

## Shape

- **Weeks 1–8**: one artifact per week, seven posts orbiting it in the weekly
  promotion arc (tease → drop → application → objection → proof → operator →
  activation).
- **Days 57–60**: the offer week. No new artifact. Recap the ladder, show the
  receipts, restate the standing offer plainly.

## 8-Week Ladder

Map artifacts to the campaign arc — each phase's artifacts do that phase's
work:

### Weeks 1–2: Reposition
Artifacts that install the worldview: standards, scoring rubrics, teardown
kits. They name the enemy and give the reader the lens. Posts carry the
thesis and the point of view.

### Weeks 3–4: Prove
Artifacts that quantify: calculators, worksheets, diagnostics. They make the
problem measurable on the reader's own numbers — evidence over assertion.
Week 4's offer bridge points at the offer directly (mid-campaign
conversion window).

### Weeks 5–6: Productize
Artifacts that equip: audits, fill-in templates, operating routines. The
reader starts RUNNING pieces of the method. Method and product education
posts live here.

### Weeks 7–8: Activate
Artifacts that assemble: the capstone playbook (weeks 1–6 assembled into the
operating system) and the offer-adjacent worksheet (the self-serve version of
the offer, one rung below it). Comment-to-DM and meeting asks peak here.

## Archetype mapping

Weekly roles map onto the stable taxonomy (`references/post-taxonomy.md`):

- `tease` → `diagnostic_framework` or `contrarian_pov`
- `drop` → `resource_guide`
- `application` → `tactical_blueprint`
- `objection` → `contrarian_pov`
- `proof` → `results_case_study`
- `operator` → `founder_story` or `feature_announcement`
- `activation` → `audience_activation` or `community_milestone`

### Recommended mix (60 rows)

The weekly arc (8 × 7 roles) plus the 4 offer-week posts yields:

- 9 `contrarian_pov` (8 objections + 1 offer-week)
- 9 `tactical_blueprint` (8 applications + 1 offer-week)
- 9 `results_case_study` (8 proofs + 1 offer-week)
- 8 `resource_guide` (the 8 drops)
- 8 `diagnostic_framework` (the 8 teases)
- 7 `audience_activation` (activations + the standing-offer post)
- 6 `founder_story`
- 2 `feature_announcement`
- 2 `community_milestone`

Days 57–60: typically `results_case_study` (receipts recap), `resource_guide`
(toolkit index), `contrarian_pov` or `founder_story` (the thesis, restated
from experience), `resource_guide` or `audience_activation` (the standing
offer).

## CTA discipline

- `drop` posts: `comment_keyword` (one keyword per artifact) or `visit_link`.
- `activation` posts: `comment_prompt`.
- `proof` posts may use `dm_me` with the offer keyword — at most one per week.
- Everything else: `comment_prompt` or `soft_reflection`. External links only
  on drops and in Featured.
- One CTA per post, ever.

## Quality rules

- No repeated hooks. Hooks come from the artifact's sharpest claims —
  thresholds, failure modes, contrarian standards.
- No more than 2 consecutive posts from the same archetype.
- Every post passes the four kill-filler tests in
  `references/artifact-system.md` before `post_ready: true`.
- Every 2-week block contains: ≥2 proof posts, ≥2 application posts, ≥1
  operator post, ≥1 conversion-oriented post (offer keyword or drop).
- Posts in a week must EXTRACT from that week's artifact — quote its checks,
  thresholds, formulas, or fields. The artifact is the source of
  specificity; a post that shares only a vibe with its artifact is filler.

## Voice grounding

Every post is written in the profile owner's observed voice:

- reuse their strongest observed hook styles (see `metrics.engagement` and
  the activity review) instead of stock LinkedIn-isms
- reuse their real proof points; never fabricate numbers, clients, or
  outcomes
- if a post needs proof the evidence doesn't contain, flag it via
  `proof_required` / `needs_review` — write around the gap, never over it
- respect the voice and style guide produced in the rewrite step
